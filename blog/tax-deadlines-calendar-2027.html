Tax Deadlines Calendar 2027 for Freelancers and Small Business Owners

Every date self-employed taxpayers need on their calendar in one place, from quarterly payments to filing deadlines.

TT Tax Tools Editorial Team · January 18, 2027 · 6 min read · 602 words
Share:

Missing a single quarterly tax deadline can trigger a penalty even if you pay everything you owe by April — self-employed taxpayers are held to a different, more frequent payment schedule than W-2 employees. Here is every key date freelancers and small business owners should track in 2027.

Note that these "quarters" are not evenly spaced — the second period is only two months, and the fourth stretches across four. Dates can shift slightly if they fall on a weekend or holiday, so always confirm the exact date close to the deadline. Our quarterly estimated taxes guide covers how to calculate each payment amount.

The main filing deadline

  • April 15, 2027 — Individual income tax returns (Form 1040) are generally due, along with any final balance owed for the prior tax year. This is also typically the deadline to make a prior-year SEP-IRA contribution if you haven't filed for an extension.
  • October 15, 2027 — Extended filing deadline, if you filed Form 4868 for an automatic six-month extension by the April deadline.

Remember that an extension to file is not an extension to pay — any tax owed is still due by the April deadline, with interest and penalties accruing on unpaid balances even if you've properly filed for a filing extension.

If you elected S-corp status, see our S-corp election guide for what that structure adds to your annual filing obligations.

Planning tip: Set calendar reminders a full week before each deadline, not on the day itself — bank transfers and estimated payment portals occasionally have processing delays that can push a same-day payment past the cutoff.

State deadlines vary

This calendar covers federal deadlines. Many states follow the same schedule for income tax and estimated payments, but not all do, and some states have their own quarterly payment thresholds or franchise tax deadlines separate from the federal calendar. Check your specific state's department of revenue for confirmation.

Frequently asked questions

What happens if I miss a quarterly estimated payment deadline?

You may owe an underpayment penalty calculated based on how late and how large the missed payment was, even if you fully pay your balance by the April filing deadline. Paying late is generally better than not paying at all, since the penalty accrues based on the shortfall.

Do these dates change every year?

The general pattern (mid-April, mid-June, mid-September, mid-January) stays consistent, but exact dates shift slightly year to year based on weekends and holidays — always verify the current year's specific dates rather than assuming last year's calendar applies exactly.

Is there a deadline to fund a Solo 401(k) I want to use this filing season?

Generally, a Solo 401(k) needs to be opened by December 31 of the tax year it applies to, even though the actual contribution deadline can extend closer to your filing deadline — see our SEP-IRA vs. Solo 401(k) guide for the distinction.
