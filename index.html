    Frequently Asked Questions

    Before you run the numbers

    Can I claim the home office deduction if I work remotely for a company?

    No, under current laws, W-2 employees cannot claim the home office deduction. It is strictly reserved for self-employed individuals. Read the full breakdown in our Home Office Deduction Guide.

    What is the standard mileage rate deduction for this year?

    The IRS adjusts it annually. Enter your total business miles driven into the calculator above to see the current-year dollar deduction instantly, or read Standard Mileage Rate vs. Actual Expenses for the full comparison against tracking actual costs.

    What happens if my itemized deductions are lower than the standard deduction?

    The calculator will automatically recommend the Standard Deduction to secure the largest tax reduction — there is never a scenario where a smaller itemized total is the better choice.

    What receipts do I need to keep to back up my self-employed expenses?

    Keep digital or physical logs of any purchase directly related to your business operation for at least three to seven years. See our full system in Record Keeping for Freelancers.
